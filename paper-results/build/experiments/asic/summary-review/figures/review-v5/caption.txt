Historical ResNet speedup is 1.08x, and the separate modified-core comparison has a normalized CGRA area of 0.91. Both pairs normalize their ASIC baseline to one; speedup uses the 1024-output ResNet block against the independent Add/ReLU ASIC with on-chip exchange, whereas area compares the modified CGRA against all seven expanded ASIC cores, including optional SAD. The performance and area results belong to different hardware revisions and do not establish a same-design performance/area improvement. Core area includes local/configuration storage but excludes endpoint adapters, DMA and external SPM; common-frequency and equal-throughput validation remain incomplete.
